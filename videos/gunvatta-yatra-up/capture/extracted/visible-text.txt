Gunvatta Yatra — Uttar Pradesh (गुणवत्ता यात्रा, उत्तर प्रदेश)
An initiative of the Quality Council of India (QCI) to promote quality excellence and awareness.
Context: QCI's nationwide Gunvatta Yatra awareness programmes cover ZED (Zero Effect Zero Defect) and LEAN certifications for MSMEs and quality practices across sectors. In March 2023 UP and QCI launched the Uttar Pradesh Gunvatta Sankalp (UP Quality Mission) — UP was the first state chosen — focusing on quality in healthcare, education, skilling and MSMEs; vision: from "Aatmanirbhar Pradesh" to "Daata Pradesh".
Video: journey map across UP — illustrative stops Lucknow, Kanpur, Varanasi, Agra, Gorakhpur — each stop a quality theme (MSME/ZED & LEAN, healthcare, education, skilling, citizens/consumers), ending with a call to join the Yatra.
