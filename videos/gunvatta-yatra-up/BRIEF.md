## Intent

A 60-second journey-map film for QCI's Gunvatta Yatra in Uttar Pradesh: an animated route
across UP, each stop revealing a quality theme (MSME / ZED, healthcare, education, skilling),
ending on a call to join. Hindi + English mix — Hindi headlines (गुणवत्ता यात्रा) with English
supporting text. Music + kinetic text only, no voiceover; must read muted on screens and at events.

## Customizations

- Journey map: illustrative stops Lucknow, Kanpur, Varanasi, Agra, Gorakhpur (no dates/figures).

## Notes

- No official logos supplied: set "Quality Council of India" and "गुणवत्ता यात्रा" as typographic marks.
