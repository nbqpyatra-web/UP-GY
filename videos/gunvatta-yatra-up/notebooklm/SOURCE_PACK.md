## 1. What Gunvatta Yatra UP is

Gunvatta Yatra (गुणवत्ता यात्रा) is a statewide quality drive in Uttar Pradesh, presented by the **Quality Council of India (QCI)**. Its focus is healthcare quality. Two raths (Rath A and Rath B) travel across the state holding **NABH Entry Level Certification workshops** (4 PM – 8 PM) for hospitals empanelled under **Ayushman Bharat – Pradhan Mantri Jan Arogya Yojana (PM-JAY)**. The aim is to take quality to every district, every city and every pincode of UP (हर ज़िला · हर शहर · हर पिनकोड).

## 2. The SACHIS circular — the reason for the Yatra

**Issued by:** State Health Agency (SACHIS), Ayushman Bharat – PM-JAY, Lucknow
**Date:** 14 May 2026
**Signed by:** Archana Verma, Chief Executive Officer
**Addressed to:** Managers and Directors of all empanelled private hospitals under the scheme, Uttar Pradesh. Copies went to the Additional Chief Secretary (Medical Health, Family Welfare and Medical Education), all District Magistrates and all Chief Medical Officers of UP.

**Subject:** Applying NABH certification so that all private hospitals empanelled under PM-JAY provide high-quality medical services.

**Key points (summarised from the Hindi original):**
1. The core aim of PM-JAY is to give beneficiaries quality, standard healthcare. Every empanelled private hospital must therefore follow the prescribed quality standards.
2. Geographic analysis and physical inspections found that some empanelled hospitals are **not meeting the minimum standards** set out in the guidelines.
3. Under the National Health Authority (Government of India) guidelines, *"Guidelines on AB PM-JAY Quality Indicators for Quality of Care"* (August 2020), quality certification is used to ensure quality services, and **quality is linked with incentives**.
4. Improving the quality of empanelled private hospitals is therefore essential. **NABH certification is mandatory** for all empanelled private hospitals, so that beneficiaries get:
   1. better quality of health services (स्वास्थ्य सेवाओं की गुणवत्ता में सुधार)
   2. treatment that follows the standards (मरीज़ों की उपचार प्रक्रिया मानकों के अनुरूप)
   3. better and more transparent services (बेहतर एवं पारदर्शी सेवाएं)
5. **Deadline:** within **2 months**, all already-empanelled private hospitals that are not NABH certified, and all new hospitals applying for empanelment, must complete NABH certification. This ensures high-quality care to NABH (National Accreditation Board for Hospitals & Healthcare Providers) standards.

## 4. Messaging rules

- QCI presents the video. You may mention NABH, Ayushman Bharat PM-JAY and SACHIS. Do not suggest that any other organisation produced it.
- **Do not quote hospital counts or other statistics** (no PM-JAY hospital totals, NABH totals, gaps or lead figures). Allowed numbers: 63 yatras, 75 districts, 2 raths, the dates, the 2-month deadline and 4–8 PM.
- Core line: **"गुणवत्ता — हर द्वार तक"** (Quality — at every doorstep). Emotional anchor: **"भरोसा"** (trust): quality is not just a certificate, it is trust.
